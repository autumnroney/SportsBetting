How to read this page
  • Green = the sportsbook paying the most for that side right now. That's where to place the bet.
  • Moneyline: +150 means a $100 bet wins $150; −150 means you bet $150 to win $100. Bigger number = better payout.
  • Spread: more points for your team is better (+7.5 beats +7). If the points match, the better price wins.
  • Total: for Over, a lower line is better; for Under, a higher line is better.
  • Value compares the best price to the market's fair odds (all books combined, vig removed). Positive value means that price beats the market consensus.
  • ▲ ▼ arrows show a price that just moved. Faded prices haven't been updated by the book recently. Always confirm the price in the sportsbook app before betting; live odds can change within seconds.
  • Untick a sportsbook above if you don't have an account there, and the "best" picks will only use your books.
